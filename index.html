The problem

In a crisis, people don't know what to do or where to go

🧭

Scattered information

Guides, shelter lists and alerts live on dozens of different sites.

⏳

Outdated data

Nobody knows if a point still has water, food or space right now.

📵

No connection

When networks fail, online-only guides become useless.

The solution

One place for guides, a live map and community reports

📘

Scenario guides

Clear Before / During / After steps and checklists for each emergency.

🗺️

Safe-places map

Shelters, food and water points, medical help, and red danger zones.

📸

Fresh community reports

Users add photos and confirm what is available right now.

📴

Offline mode (next)

Guides and map data saved on the phone for when the network is down.

Core feature

A live map people can trust

Layers

Shelters Food & water Medical Danger zones
  • Each point shows type, photos, last-confirmed time and confirmations
  • Filter by freshness: 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days
  • Danger zones drawn as red areas: “do not go here”
User flow

From a report to a verified point in three steps

1

Report

A signed-in user adds a photo, type and location of a shelter, supplies or a danger.

2

Review

The report starts as “pending” and goes live after moderator approval.

3

Confirm

Other people confirm it. Points without fresh confirmations fade and are marked outdated.

Trust & safety

A wrong report in a crisis is dangerous, so trust is built in

Data quality

  • Moderation before publishing
  • Multiple confirmations raise trust
  • Automatic “outdated” after 30 days
  • Abuse reporting and rate limits

Privacy & responsibility

  • Photo location metadata is removed
  • Row-level security in the database
  • Official sources shown as the baseline
  • Clear disclaimer: call 911 in an emergency
Roadmap

Start small, prove it, then scale

1 · MVP

  • Website with key guides
  • Map with official data
  • Reports, photos, moderation

2 · Pilot

  • Launch in 1–2 high-risk states
  • Verify data, gather feedback
  • Trust scores for contributors

3 · Scale

  • All US states
  • Offline PWA and alerts
  • Partnerships with agencies and NGOs
